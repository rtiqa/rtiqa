# Code of Conduct

RTIQA is committed to creating a welcoming and inclusive community for everyone. All participants are expected to treat others with respect, professionalism, and empathy.

## Our standards

All interactions should be:

- respectful
- constructive
- inclusive
- professional

We do not tolerate:

- harassment, bullying, or abusive language
- discrimination or hateful conduct
- threats or intimidation
- personal attacks
- harassment based on race, religion, gender, sexual orientation, disability, or any other characteristic

## Expected behavior

- Be kind and patient.
- Assume good intent.
- Provide feedback constructively.
- Respect different backgrounds and perspectives.
- Help create a safe environment for collaboration.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Using offensive or derogatory language.
- Insulting or demeaning others.
- Making discriminatory comments.
- Sharing personal attacks or threats.
- Disrupting discussions intentionally.

## Reporting and enforcement

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please report it to the project maintainers.

Reports may be submitted using the contact methods in `SUPPORT.md`. Maintainers will review reports promptly and may take action, including:

- private warnings
- temporary suspension from projects or channels
- removal from community spaces
- reporting to platform administrators

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces, including:

- GitHub issues and pull requests
- comments and discussions
- chat and messaging platforms
- community meetings and events
